Steam Library

Leave the root blank and KOVA auto-detects your Steam install.

Standalone Game Folders

Folders KOVA scans for games that didn't come from a store — .app bundles, .exe files, AppImages, Linux binaries. When this list is empty, KOVA checks ~/Games and ~/KOVA Games instead.

Library Visibility

Games you hide (from their game page) disappear from Home, the Library, and the Quick Panel. Hiding is always reversible — hidden games are listed here with an Unhide button.

Quick Panel

Press ⌘+Shift+K anywhere to summon the KOVA Quick Panel — your library and Quick Resume, floating over the desktop. Esc closes it.
